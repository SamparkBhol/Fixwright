HONEST CAVEATS
  Generated scenarios. The 700 dev and held-out scenarios come from homefault's generator, which uses
  the same 10 cause library as Fixwright's engine and was written by the same team. The held-out seeds
  (10001 to 10200) were kept for reporting, but held-out smoke runs were seen during the build, so they
  are not untouched. The setup favours Fixwright: its probes are made for exactly these 10 causes.
  Reconstructed incidents. The 20 incidents are hand reconstructions of dated public reports into Home
  Assistant shaped logs, with stated assumptions where a report names no cause. They are not recordings
  of real homes, and 20 is too few to decide anything on their own.
  Unanswered calls. None: every B1 and B2 call got an answer from the LLM server.
  Simulated person. Answers come from each scenario's ground truth, wrong 10% of the time, not from people.
  One model. B1 and B2 use one open weights model (gpt-oss-20b, MXFP4 MoE) on CPU with reasoning effort low
  and a 1024 token cap, called once per scenario with no tools. A larger model, more reasoning or an LLM
  that can run probes may do better; this run does not measure that.
  One seed. One stratified sample of 100 held-out scenarios, one person seed, one model seed.
  Lenient kill switch. Interval overlap counts as a match, so the switch fires whenever the data cannot
  tell the agents apart. It stays off only when Fixwright leads by a gap larger than both intervals allow.
  Router. The live product's router sends some questions to explain_event; on all 720 it produced 21 of
  Fixwright's 24 false confirmations (true causes: dead_trigger).
  Threads. Answers were reproducible at temperature 0 with one slot and no prompt cache in bench/llm's
  check; this run used 8 threads, and a different thread count can change floating point sums and so
  answers. Not measured here.

WHAT THESE NUMBERS DO NOT SHOW
  That Fixwright finds faults in real homes with real devices and real people.
  Whether people answer "Did it blink?" as reliably as the simulated person.
  Repairs: HomeTruth scores the diagnosis only, not the dry run, apply, read back or rollback.
  Voice latency, Alexa+ integration, or how the answers sound.
  How a frontier LLM, or an LLM agent with probe tools, would compare.
  Whether the 10 causes cover the faults real homes have.
